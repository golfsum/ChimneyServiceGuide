# SEO Strategy — chimneyserviceguide.com

## Positioning

Guide-heavy content site with quote-matching funnel. SEO measured against leads and guide engagement.

Do **not** mass-generate thin `service + city` doorway pages. Publish city/service pages only when they have unique local and service-specific content.

## URL architecture

- Services: `/{service}`
- Guides: `/{guide-slug}`
- City hub: `/{state}/{city}`
- City + service: `/{state}/{city}/{service}`

## Index policy

- `indexable` — launch cluster above
- `draft` — remaining services until unique content is written
- `noindex` — admin, utility, thin variants

## Content principles

1. Guides should stand alone as useful reading (creosote, sweep intervals, fire signs).
2. Every indexable page ends with a quote CTA.
3. Internal links connect guides ↔ services ↔ Tucson cluster.
